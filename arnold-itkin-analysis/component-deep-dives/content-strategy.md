## E-E-A-T Content Strategy

### Experience (First-Hand)

**Demonstrated Through**:
- Named case results with details
- Specific verdict/settlement amounts
- Client testimonials with full names
- Attorney involvement in specific cases

**Example**:
> "Our attorneys secured $8 billion for families affected by the PG&E wildfires."

### Expertise

**Demonstrated Through**:
- Detailed practice area explanations
- Legal process breakdowns
- Industry-specific knowledge (offshore, trucking)
- Attorney credentials and specializations

### Authoritativeness

**Demonstrated Through**:
- $20 billion recovered total
- Record-setting verdicts
- Media coverage (CNN, Forbes, WSJ)
- Industry awards (Best Lawyers, Super Lawyers)

### Trustworthiness

**Demonstrated Through**:
- Real client testimonials with names
- Transparent fee information ("no fee unless we win")
- Physical office locations
- Bar admissions and license numbers
- Detailed attorney backgrounds
