## Quantified Trust Signals

### Numbers That Build Trust

| Metric | Value | Impact |
|--------|-------|--------|
| Total recovered | $20 Billion | Scale of success |
| Largest verdict | $8 Billion | Record-setting ability |
| Years in practice | 20+ | Experience |
| Attorneys on staff | 30+ | Resources/capacity |
| Office locations | 6 | Accessibility |
| States served | 3+ | Reach |

### Specificity = Credibility

**Weak**: "We've won millions for our clients"
**Strong**: "$20 billion recovered, including an $8 billion verdict"

**Weak**: "Award-winning attorneys"
**Strong**: "Best Lawyers in America 2016-2026, Inner Circle of Advocates"

**Weak**: "Experienced team"
**Strong**: "Kurt Arnold - 20+ years, license #24036150"

## Competitor Differentiation Through Trust

### What Sets Them Apart

1. **Scale of Results**
   - Most law firms can't claim billion-dollar verdicts
   - $20B total creates perception of "best in class"

2. **National Recognition**
   - Media logos suggest legitimacy beyond local market
   - Implies handling of high-profile cases

3. **Transparency**
   - Specific dollar amounts (not ranges)
   - Named clients in testimonials
   - Attorney license numbers displayed

4. **Consistency**
   - Same trust signals on every page
   - Reinforced through repetition
   - Multiple formats (text, logos, video)

## Trust Signal Opportunities (Gaps)

### Not Currently Implemented

1. **Review Aggregation**
   - No visible Google review count
   - No Avvo/Martindale ratings displayed
   - Could add AggregateRating schema

2. **Case Studies**
   - Case results exist but lack detailed narratives
   - Could create in-depth case study pages
   - Would support long-tail keywords

3. **Client Count**
   - "5,000+ clients served" type metric
   - Adds scale beyond just dollars

4. **Success Rate**
   - "98% case success rate"
   - Would need to be verifiable

5. **Response Time**
   - "We respond within 24 hours"
   - "Same-day consultations available"
