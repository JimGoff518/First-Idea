# Investment Proposal: Digital Market Dominance Initiative

**Goff Law, PLLC**
**Prepared**: January 2026
**Investment Amount**: $10,000,000
**Investment Period**: 36 Months

---

## Executive Summary

This proposal outlines a strategic $10 million investment to transform Goff Law from a regional Dallas personal injury firm into the dominant digital presence across the Texas personal injury market.

**The Opportunity**: Our primary competitor, Arnold & Itkin, has built a $20B verdict portfolio partly through digital dominance (602 web pages, 312 blog posts, 6 office locations). With strategic investment, Goff Law can achieve comparable—or superior—digital market share in the DFW metroplex and expand statewide.

**The Ask**: $10 million over 36 months allocated across digital infrastructure, content production, media acquisition, office expansion, and talent acquisition.

**The Return**: Projected 5-10x ROI through increased case acquisition, higher-value case selection, and market positioning as the premier Texas personal injury firm.

## Risk Assessment

### High-Impact Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| Google algorithm change | Medium | High | Diversified traffic sources, white-hat SEO only |
| Increased competition | High | Medium | First-mover advantage, brand building |
| Rising ad costs | High | Medium | Shift budget to organic as rankings improve |
| Economic downturn | Low | Medium | PI demand often increases in downturns |
| Key personnel departure | Medium | Medium | Documentation, cross-training, retention packages |

### Mitigation Strategies

1. **Diversification**: No single channel exceeds 40% of leads
2. **Brand Building**: Long-term asset that survives algorithm changes
3. **Content Ownership**: All content owned, not rented (vs. paid ads)
4. **Talent Retention**: Competitive compensation, equity consideration
5. **Phased Investment**: Annual review gates before next-year funding
